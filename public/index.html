Inattention Blindness Experiment

Inattention blindness (and change blindness) is the psychological phenomenon where observers fail to notice a significant visual change when a brief disruption—such as a blank screen or eye blink—masks the natural motion cues that ordinarily draw focal attention.

Phase 01

Initial Stimulus

An intricate visual scene is presented for a brief duration. Absorb the entire composition.

Phase 02

Black Screen (Mask)

A momentary black screen disrupts ocular motion transients, clearing retinal persistence.

Phase 03

Spot The Change

The scene reappears with a single element altered. Click directly on the changed area as fast as possible.
